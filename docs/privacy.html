Privacy Policy

Chrome extension “WA Translate – AI translator for WhatsApp Web” · Effective: November 20, 2026

1. Summary

WA Translate translates messages on web.whatsapp.com. We (the developer) do not run any server, do not collect your data, and do not use analytics or advertising. Everything the extension stores stays in your browser. Message text leaves your browser only to the translation provider you choose in the extension (using your own API key), and only for chats in which you turned translation on.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not operate a backend; no data is sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, other than sending message text to the translation provider you chose, which is necessary for the extension’s single purpose (translation).
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension’s single purpose, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No humans at our side read your messages.
  • The extension does not execute remotely hosted code.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-party providers

When message text is sent to a provider, that provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy (for API customers these often include limited retention for abuse monitoring, and may differ for free tiers — for example, free tiers of some providers may use data to improve their models). Please review the policy of the provider you pick:

  • Anthropic — anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • OpenAI — openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google (Gemini API, Google Translate) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • xAI — x.ai/legal/privacy-policy
  • OpenRouter — openrouter.ai/privacy
  • DeepL — deepl.com/privacy

5. Permissions the extension uses

  • Access to web.whatsapp.com — to read the text of messages in the open chat, show translations, and (when you confirm) put the translated text into the message box and press Send.
  • storage — to save settings, keys and the cache locally.
  • Optional access to provider API servers — requested one provider at a time, only when you select that provider (or enable the Google fallback).

6. Your choices and control

  • Translation is off by default and works only in chats you enable. Turn it off any time (chat header button, popup, or the master switch).
  • Delete the cache with “Clear”; remove all stored data (settings, keys, cache) by uninstalling the extension or clearing the extension’s storage.
  • Revoke provider access at chrome://extensions → WA Translate → Details → Site access / permissions.

7. Children, changes, contact

The extension is not directed to children under 13. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version published at this address. Questions: GitHub issues: github.com/fls-dev/wa_translate_store.
